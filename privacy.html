Privacy Policy: Blast & Restore

Developer: Steady Hand Games (support@steadyhandgames.com)  ·  Effective date: October 17, 2026

This policy covers the Blast & Restore game for Android and the web version at this site.

No accounts. Your progress stays on your device.

The game has no sign-up or login. Your progress, settings, favorite colors and unlocked items are saved only on your device (local app storage). We don't run servers that collect or store this data. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, that progress is deleted.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app offers optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Ads only play when you choose to watch one for an in-game boost. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process device information. This includes your device's advertising ID, IP address, general (coarse) location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you'll be asked for consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (such as Remove Ads and premium paint finishes) are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play gives the app a record of what you own so purchases can be unlocked and restored. See the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

We don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. We don't sell personal data, and we use no analytics beyond what AdMob itself does as described above.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
